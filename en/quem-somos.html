Direction

Mission, vision, and purpose

Mission

Technical and engineering solutions for the transition

To be the leading Brazilian provider of global technical and engineering services focused on renewable energy, water solutions, environmental management, and sustainable infrastructure.

Vision

Benchmark hub for the energy transition

To be recognized as a benchmark hub for the energy transition and sustainable ecosystems, driving socioeconomic transformation, technological development, and lasting positive impact for future generations.

Purpose

Infrastructure that creates opportunity

A world where infrastructure unlocks opportunities for everyone. We bring together people, ideas, and technical expertise to turn our partners’ ambitions into executable projects.

Culture

Conscious leadership applied to real-world challenges

At Eco Green, a culture of innovation drives solutions to the defining industrial and environmental challenges of our time. We bring together a multidisciplinary ecosystem uniting scientists, engineers, and sector leaders.

That discipline runs from concept design through final execution of every asset. We treat our partners’ challenges as our own, delivering practical, effective, and cost-efficient solutions.

Technical capability

Disciplined project management and a commitment to safety and quality across every workstream.

People as core asset

Entrepreneurial drive and commitment to shared success create growth and opportunity across the team.

Focus on partner outcomes

Technical and operational excellence with predictable project execution, from field to boardroom.

Values

What defines how we act

Six commitments guiding technical, commercial, and governance decisions across the company.

01

Safety, ethics, and quality first

We operate with integrity and ethics, upholding safety and quality across everything we do.

02

Collaborate to win

We unite specialized knowledge from diverse backgrounds to anticipate and solve our partners’ toughest challenges.

03

Innovate with impact

We embrace fresh ideas and build solutions that create value for partners, communities, and employees.

04

Sustain our world

We act to generate positive planetary impact, enrich surrounding communities, and leave a legacy for future generations.

05

Thrive together

We foster a welcoming environment, engaged teams, and fair opportunities for every person to reach their potential.

06

Deliver excellence

We grow the business with relentless partner focus, technical rigor, and outstanding project execution.

Governance

How decisions are made and overseen

Eco Polo Nordeste’s governance structure combines corporate oversight, an integrity program, and formal risk management.

Board of Directors

Oversees the Corporate Governance Program, including the Ethics and Compliance Program and ESG policies, procedures, and reporting.

Sustainability Directorate

Leads the Sustainability Program and delivers quarterly reports to the Board of Directors.

Sustainability Council

Reporting to the Sustainability Directorate, coordinates and monitors the program and embeds best practices across the organization.

Ethics, compliance & risk

Our Ethics and Compliance Program, corporate policies, and risk management framework uphold conduct standards with employees, partners, and clients.
