Data Protection Officer (DPO)

{{site_legal_name}} maintains a public, operational channel for communications with the Data Protection Officer (Encarregado de Dados / DPO), serving data subjects and Brazil’s National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

Official channel: {{site_email_legal}} — please use the subject line “LGPD — Data Protection Officer”.

What the company never does

Eco Polo Nordeste never requests payments, deposits, registration fees, or banking details to participate in recruitment processes, supplier onboarding, or complex occupancy. Any message making such requests should be reported to {{site_email_legal}}.

Art. 46 & 48 of the LGPD

Information security and incidents

Technical website safeguards

Traffic served exclusively over encrypted HTTPS across the domain, hardened HTTP security response headers, protected session attributes, and zero secrets embedded in browser-facing code.

Data minimization

Forms collect only the minimum needed for their stated purpose. There is no hidden collection, no third-party tracker loaded before consent, and no automatic loading of embedded widgets.

Incident notification

In the event of a security incident posing relevant risk or harm to data subjects, the company will notify Brazil’s ANPD and affected individuals within a reasonable timeframe, detailing data involved, risks, and mitigation measures.

Report a vulnerability

Security researchers may report findings to {{site_email_legal}}. We ask for responsible disclosure: please allow time for remediation prior to public release.
