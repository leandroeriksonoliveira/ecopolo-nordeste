Thesis

Why Northeast Brazil, why now

Resource

Concentrated renewable endowment

Northeast Brazil combines conditions for utility-scale renewable generation and contiguous land availability in the same territory — a rare combination for energy-dense projects.

Demand

Loads the public grid cannot absorb

Hyperscale data centers, electrolysis, bioenergy, and intensive industry compete for grid interconnection and water. The complex delivers both on a dedicated basis, outside the queue.

Structure

Shared infrastructure

Power, water, effluent treatment, logistics, and environmental governance shared across anchors and satellites, lowering individual CAPEX for each tenant company.

Impact

Inland development with social license

Deployment in the Alto Sertão of Alagoas paired with education, family-farming, and entrepreneurship programs building local community alignment from day one.

Stage

Where the project stands today

The project is in the planning and structuring phase following its official launch. For evaluating parties, that means Phase 1 scope is defined and governance is formalized, while live operations and installed capacity are not yet active.

The company does not represent as closed anything contingent on permitting, detailed engineering, or contract signature. That principle also applies to all project figures — read the methodology note.
