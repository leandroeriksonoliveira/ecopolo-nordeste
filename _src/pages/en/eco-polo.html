Transparency

How to read the figures on this website

Eco Polo Nordeste is a project in the planning and permitting stage. That carries a practical consequence we prefer to state openly: published figures are design targets, not measured operating outcomes or installed capacity.

  • Power, area, and company counts reflect current engineering sizing and may change with permitting, executive engineering, and contracting.
  • Efficiency metrics (PUE and WUE) are design targets. Operational figures will only be published once measured in live facilities, with declared scope and reporting period.
  • Schedule. Phases advance alongside permitting, earthworks, and contracts. We publish confirmed milestones and do not publish dates dependent on external approvals.
  • Environmental claims. We do not present environmental results as guaranteed. Any future claim of reduced emissions, consumption, or impact will include methodology, scope, and measurement period.

Where legacy materials diverge from figures on this website, the data published here prevails. Investors and technical partners seeking source traceability may request details via our investor relations channel.

Strategic sectors

Who the complex is built to host

An integrated, intelligent ecosystem dedicated to catalyzing the growth of technology and sustainability leaders. Among the projects presented at launch are the three below.

Hyperscale data centers

Hub dedicated to artificial intelligence workloads, powered by off-grid renewable energy and closed-loop water cooling.

Bioenergy & biofuels

Millennium Bioenergia corn ethanol plant among the anchor projects presented, integrated with the complex’s biomass streams.

Electric mobility

Electric truck assembly plant serving regional freight transport and supporting logistics decarbonization across Northeast Brazil.

Projects publicly presented at the complex’s launch. Executed contractual status for each project is verified case by case under investor relations.
