1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website. Equivalent storage technologies — such as localStorage, sessionStorage, and pixels — are governed identically under this policy.

First-party cookies are set by this domain. Third-party cookies are set by external domains, typically via embedded content. On this site, third-party content loads only upon your explicit authorization.

2. Categories we use

Strictly necessary — always active

Essential for core site operation: security, integrity, and remembering your cookie decision. Because the site cannot function properly without them, they do not require opt-in consent. Legal basis: legitimate interest to deliver the requested service (Art. 7, IX, of the LGPD).

Preferences — optional

Remember display choices such as language and collapsed UI sections. Legal basis: consent (Art. 7, I).

Analytics — optional

Aggregated audience measurement with data minimization to understand which pages are useful. Legal basis: consent (Art. 7, I).

Marketing — optional

Campaign measurement and embedded third-party content such as interactive maps and videos. Legal basis: consent (Art. 7, I).

4. Embedded third-party content

The interactive map on the contact page is the sole third-party embed on the site. It is gated behind a consent placeholder so zero requests reach the map provider prior to your explicit action.

Links to our LinkedIn and Instagram profiles are standard hyperlinks: they load no third-party scripts and set no cookies while you remain on this website.

5. How we log your consent

Each decision creates a record with: unique identifier, ISO 8601 timestamp, consent notice version, decision method (accept-all, reject-non-essential, or custom), authorized categories, page path, and browser language.

The receipt is kept in your browser and viewable above. Once a server consent endpoint is configured, a copy is sent to the company’s log for audit proof. The log contains no name, email, or direct personal identifier.

If the consent notice version changes, fresh consent will be requested.

6. Browser controls

In addition to this site’s preference panel, you can block or clear cookies in your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Safari: Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data.
  • Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data.
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions.

In private browsing mode, choices apply only to that session.
