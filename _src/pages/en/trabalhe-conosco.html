Careers

Build the complex with us

We are looking for people ready to turn sustainable vision into real industry in Northeast Brazil. Experience and fresh ideas belong together here: we value cross-generational collaboration and actively combat ageism and gender inequality.

Transparency

What happens to your CV

Hiring workflows often hide this information. Here it is stated up front before you submit any personal data.

01

Receipt

Your profile enters a talent pool accessed strictly by our people team and relevant hiring managers.

02

Screening

We review fit across workstreams under structuring. Profiles without an immediate match stay in the pool.

03

24-month retention

We retain your record for up to 24 months from submission to consider you for upcoming roles.

04

Deletion

At the end of the retention period, or upon your request, data and files are permanently deleted with an audit log.

You may leave the talent pool at any time

To request confirmation, access, correction, portability, or deletion of your personal data, write to {{site_email_legal}} or use our data subject rights form. Our response deadline is 15 days.

Talent pool

Submit your CV

Share only what is necessary. We ask for your name, contact details, target role level, and your CV file — nothing more.

Do not include sensitive personal data not required for a position, such as health information, racial or ethnic origin, religious beliefs, political opinions, union membership, or biometric data. If included in your file, such data will be processed strictly as needed for recruitment.

Accepted formats: PDF, DOC, or DOCX. Maximum file size: 2 MB.

Privacy notice for this collection
Purpose
Candidate evaluation and maintenance of a talent pool for company recruitment processes.
Data collected
Name, email, phone, target role, application level, and the attached CV file with whatever contents you choose to include.
Legal basis
Data subject consent — Art. 7, I, of the LGPD. For any sensitive data included in a CV, Art. 11, I, of the LGPD (specific and highlighted consent).
Retention period
Up to 24 months from submission. Thereafter, records and files are permanently deleted.
Disposal procedure
Deletion of the record and file from company systems, with an operational deletion log kept for proof.
Sharing
People team, hiring managers, and, where applicable, contracted recruitment advisors under controller instruction. No sale or transfer to third parties.
Automated decision-making
We do not use solely automated decision-making to screen candidates.
