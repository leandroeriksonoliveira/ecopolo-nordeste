1. Commitment

We strive to ensure everyone can use this website, with or without assistive technology, on any device. Accessibility checks for semantics, contrast, focus indicators, and keyboard operation are integrated into our development workflow before release.

Art. 63 of Brazilian Law 13,146/2015 (Brazilian Inclusion Law) requires websites maintained by companies with headquarters or commercial representation in Brazil to be accessible to persons with disabilities. This statement is our public commitment.

2. Declared conformance level

Substantially conformant with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. Exceptions and future third-party verification items are listed transparently in Section 4.

This statement covers all Portuguese and English pages on this domain in version {{site_policy_version}}. Content hosted on external platforms — such as LinkedIn and Instagram — is governed by those platforms’ accessibility standards.

3. Implemented features

  • Semantic structure: landmark regions (header, nav, main, footer), sequential heading hierarchy without skipped levels, and proper list markup.
  • Skip link: “Skip to main content” as the first focusable control on every page.
  • Keyboard navigation: all controls — menus, carousel tabs, disclosures, forms, language switcher, and cookie banner — are keyboard-operable in logical focus order.
  • Visible focus: high-contrast 3 px focus outline across all interactive controls, never suppressed.
  • Contrast: text and UI components verified to meet at least 4.5:1 (normal text) and 3:1 (large text and controls), avoiding low-contrast white-on-gold combinations.
  • Text alternatives: informative images and diagrams carry descriptive alt text; decorative visuals are marked accordingly for screen readers.
  • Forms: explicit labels, screen-reader required cues, field-linked error messages, and aria-live status announcements.
  • External links: links opening in a new tab are announced in their accessible name.
  • Motion: transitions respect prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Zoom and responsiveness: relative units tested up to 200% zoom and from 320 px mobile to ultrawide viewports without horizontal scrolling.
  • Progressive enhancement: all core content and navigation remain accessible even with JavaScript disabled.
  • Language tags: declared page language (pt-BR / en) and hreflang alternates, plus expanded abbr titles.

4. Known limitations

We openly disclose remaining limitations and mitigations:

  • Third-party embedded content. The contact page map is served by OpenStreetMap, whose internal iframe controls are outside our direct control. Mitigation: full postal address and route links remain permanently visible in text, and the map embed is optional.
  • Downloadable documents. Future PDF downloads may not initially be fully tagged for screen readers. Mitigation: accessible formats are provided upon request via Section 6.
  • Independent third-party audit. Automated and manual accessibility verification for this release was performed internally (including Lighthouse audits). An independent third-party audit with a publishable report is planned.

5. How we evaluate

Verification for this release combined:

  • automated HTML structure, heading, label, and Lighthouse Accessibility audits;
  • full keyboard-only navigation across pages and forms;
  • color contrast verification across light and brand-green surfaces;
  • responsive layout checks from 320 px to 2560 px and 200% zoom;
  • verification with JavaScript disabled and reduced motion enabled.

Re-evaluation takes place upon every material site update and at least annually.

6. Encountered a barrier? Contact us

If any part of this website prevented or hindered your access, please let us know. Include the page URL, what you were trying to do, and any assistive technology used.
