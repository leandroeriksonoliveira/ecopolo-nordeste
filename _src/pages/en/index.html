Why we exist

Three bottlenecks hold back intensive industry in Brazil. The complex was engineered around them.

Predictable power, water availability, and social license are the critical path for any high-density operation. Eco Polo Nordeste solves all three on the same site before tenant companies arrive.

Energy security

Dedicated, off-grid renewable power supply designed with a focus on energy efficiency measured by PUE. The goal is to decouple operations from tariff volatility and grid interconnection queues.

Water resilience

Industrial water and closed-loop technologies targeting optimized WUE and reuse of treated effluents, ensuring water ceases to be a limiting factor for industrial scale.

Compliance & ESG

Structured governance, alignment with international frameworks and the UN 2030 Agenda, so tenant companies inherit a ready-made regulatory and reputational foundation.

Where the project begins

Delmiro Gouveia, in the Alto Sertão of Alagoas

The official launch of Eco Polo Nordeste took place on September 25, 2026, in Delmiro Gouveia. Choosing this municipality is not symbolic: it is a deliberate strategy to bring industry and technology inland, deploying leading-edge infrastructure where it drives the highest economic transformation per real invested.

The model enables tenant companies to share infrastructure, power, technology, expertise, and sustainable solutions — strengthening productive chains rather than merely leasing plots on a shared site.

Deployment is phased by quadrant, aligned with permitting, earthworks, and contracting progress. Confirmed milestones are published through the company’s official channels.

2030 Agenda

Sustainability is the engineering, not the pitch

The complex’s operational architecture was engineered around the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals. That shows up in design choices — energy matrix, water cycle, reverse logistics for waste — rather than merely stated pledges.

For a tenant company, the practical effect is inheriting stronger environmental indicators from day one of operations without building that layer alone. Power, water, and effluent treatment arrive pre-dimensioned.
