2. Personal data we process

We process only the data strictly necessary for each interaction. We never purchase contact lists or enrich records with data sourced from third-party brokers.

2.1 Data you provide directly

  • Newsletter: email address.
  • Commercial & institutional contact: name, company, email, phone, subject, and message content.
  • Investor relations: name, organization, email, phone (optional), stakeholder profile, and message.
  • CV submission: name, email, phone, target role, application level, and the CV file along with whatever content you choose to include in it.
  • Data subject rights request: name, email, requested right, data origin, and request details.

2.2 Data collected automatically

  • Server access logs: IP address, request timestamp, requested URL, HTTP status code, and browser user agent, maintained by hosting infrastructure for security and legal compliance where applicable under Brazil’s Internet Bill of Rights (Law 12,965/2014).
  • Cookies and identifiers: only strictly necessary storage is used without consent. Preference, analytics, and marketing categories require your prior authorization — see Section 4.

2.3 Sensitive personal data

We do not request sensitive personal data (Art. 5, II, of the LGPD) on any form on this website. If you voluntarily include sensitive data — for instance, inside an uploaded CV — it will be processed solely to the extent required for the stated purpose under Art. 11, I, of the LGPD, and may be deleted upon your request.

4. Cookies and tracking technologies

On this website, no non-essential cookie is stored prior to your choice. On your first visit, you may accept all categories, reject non-essential ones, or customize your preferences.

We log your choice with an identifier, timestamp, accepted notice version, and authorized categories so consent can be evidenced as required by Art. 8, §1, of the LGPD. You can view your receipt and change your decision at any time via Cookie Policy or the “Manage cookies” button in the footer.

Third-party embedded content, such as maps, loads only after explicit authorization. Until then, an equivalent plain-text address and route links are shown.

5. Who we share data with

We never sell, rent, or trade personal data. Sharing occurs only in the cases below and is strictly limited to what is necessary:

  • Contracted processors: hosting, email delivery, and form infrastructure providers acting exclusively under controller instructions and bound by confidentiality and security obligations.
  • Professional advisors: legal counsel, accounting, and recruitment advisors when required for the stated purpose.
  • Public authorities: upon valid legal requisition, court order, or regulatory obligation, strictly to the extent required.
  • Corporate reorganization: in the event of a merger, acquisition, or restructuring, preserving original purposes and notifying data subjects where required by law.

6. International data transfers

Parts of our hosting and email infrastructure may operate servers outside Brazil. In such cases, international transfers comply with Chapter V of the LGPD, relying on contractual safeguards ensuring protection compatible with Brazilian law.

You may request information on destination countries and applied safeguards via the contact channel in Section 12.

7. Retention periods and disposal

Retention periods are listed in Section 3. Once the period expires or the purpose is fulfilled, data is permanently deleted or irreversibly anonymized, with a deletion log retained for audit proof.

Retention may be extended solely under Art. 16 of the LGPD — compliance with a legal or regulatory obligation, anonymized research studies, legally compliant third-party transfers, or exclusive controller use with anonymized data.

For the CV talent pool, the retention limit is 24 months from submission, followed by permanent deletion, with early deletion available at any time upon request.

8. Your rights as a data subject

Art. 18 of the LGPD grants you the right, at any time and upon request, to obtain:

  • confirmation of the existence of processing;
  • access to your personal data;
  • correction of incomplete, inaccurate, or outdated data;
  • anonymization, blocking, or deletion of unnecessary, excessive, or unlawfully processed data;
  • data portability to another service or product provider, subject to commercial and industrial secrets;
  • deletion of personal data processed with your consent, subject to Art. 16 exceptions;
  • information about public and private entities with which data was shared;
  • information on the possibility of withholding consent and the consequences of refusal;
  • revocation of consent;
  • objection to processing carried out under another legal basis.

How to exercise your rights: use the data subject rights form or email {{site_email_legal}}. The response deadline is 15 calendar days from receipt. We may request additional verification details strictly to prevent unauthorized disclosure to third parties.

If you are unsatisfied with our response, you may file a complaint with Brazil’s National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) or consumer protection agencies.

9. Information security

We apply technical and administrative safeguards to protect personal data from unauthorized access and accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, or disclosure under Art. 46 of the LGPD — including HTTPS-only transport, need-to-know access controls, hardened HTTP security headers, data minimization, and zero secrets in client-side code.

In the event of a security incident that may pose relevant risk or harm to data subjects, we will notify the ANPD and affected individuals within a reasonable timeframe pursuant to Art. 48 of the LGPD.

No system is completely immune to risk. Please do not submit sensitive data, passwords, or government ID documents via unsolicited forms or emails.

10. Children and adolescents

This website is intended for adult and professional audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children or adolescents. If such data is identified without specific and highlighted parental or guardian consent, it will be deleted promptly upon notice via Section 12.

11. Changes to this policy

Where a material update affects the legal basis of previously collected consent, we will request fresh consent before continuing processing.
