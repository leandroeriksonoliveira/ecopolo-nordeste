Sustainability

Sustainability is the engineering that enables the business model

Technical efficiency, economic viability, and socio-environmental impact in one design — aligned with the UN SDGs.

Sustainability program

A framework underpinning long-term strategy

Eco Polo Nordeste’s sustainability program is a comprehensive framework supporting long-term business strategy and driving continuous improvement. Our Sustainability Policy and Sustainability Approach articulate the company’s commitments.

This approach is embedded in corporate strategy with a declared purpose: shaping a sustainable future in Northeast Brazil alongside leading global organizations.

Our exclusive focus on sustainability projects, range of services, and integrated approach — from field operations to the boardroom — allow us to work side by side with partners to tackle their most urgent challenges, whether targeted technical support or full-scale strategy.

Environmental

Ecosystem protection and restoration, biodiversity conservation, and nature-based solutions supported by real-time data collection for land, forest, and soil stewardship.

Social

Promoting well-being across surrounding communities and our workforce network, with corporate social responsibility built into the operating model.

Governance

Code of Business Conduct and Ethics, ESG policies, and Board of Directors oversight, with quarterly reports from the Sustainability Directorate.

In practice

How we operate the SDGs

Five concrete fronts, each tied directly to the goals it serves.

01 — SDG 7 & SDG 13

Clean matrix and energy efficiency

Infrastructure centered on the energy transition, using high-performance renewable sources to provide operational security for tenant industries. This cuts the carbon footprint of the hosted value chain and directly advances climate action.

  • 07Affordable and clean energy
  • 13Climate action
02 — SDG 6 & SDG 12

Water management and circular economy

Production cycles inside the complex are built on rational water use and smart reuse, combined with reverse logistics for waste and inputs. Potential environmental liabilities become operational assets inside the ecosystem.

  • 06Clean water and sanitation
  • 12Responsible consumption and production
03 — SDG 8 & SDG 9

Industrial innovation and decent work

Investing in process modernization and attracting clean technologies creates a compelling environment for international productive capital, while generating skilled jobs under fair working conditions and raising regional competitiveness.

  • 08Decent work and economic growth
  • 09Industry, innovation and infrastructure
04 — SDG 5, SDG 10 & SDG 11

Inclusion, equity, and community development

True sustainability requires respect for people. The company works to reduce inequalities, actively combats ageism, promotes gender equality, and supports social inclusion programs that strengthen surrounding communities.

  • 05Gender equality
  • 10Reduced inequalities
  • 11Sustainable cities and communities
05 — SDG 17

Collaborative governance and global alliances

No ecosystem stands alone. The company builds bridges among the private sector, public authorities, research institutions, and international investors to enable sustainable projects at scale.

  • 17Partnerships for the goals

Legacy

Sustainable development is also built through people

Initiatives announced at the complex’s official launch on September 25, 2026, in Delmiro Gouveia.

Escola IA Program

Technology and artificial intelligence training for the local education network, with reported investment exceeding R$ 5.7 million at the launch announcement.

Family farming

Initial seed donations to regional growers and technical support in crop-livestock integration and biofertilizers.

Local entrepreneurship

Initiatives strengthening entrepreneurship around the complex so the local supply chain develops within the region itself.
